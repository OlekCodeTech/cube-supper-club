CUBESupper Club

London since 2024

Ten people.
One table.

A private supper club in London. Every guest is invited personally, and the conversations don’t end with dessert.

The idea

  1. The dinner is the experience.
  2. The table is what makes it valuable.
  3. The relationships are what make it worth returning for.
  4. The network is the long-term outcome.

How it works

Not an event.
A table.

  1. I

    An invitation

    Michał invites every guest himself. A seat at Cube can’t be bought or booked, and no two tables are the same.

  2. II

    The table

    Ten people from different worlds: hospitality, fashion, art, investment, craft. Chosen so that the conversations surprise everyone, including the host.

  3. III

    Afterwards

    The dinner ends, the conversation doesn’t have to. Michał stays in touch, listens to what each guest is working on, and makes introductions when the moment is right.
